Examination Paper Custody & Leak Tracing Architecture (Concept I01)

7-STAGE PROTOCOL
1
Master Paper
University Controller of Examinations drafts canonical exam paper.
2
Multi-Layer Fingerprint
Injects Layer 1 (Zero-Width), Layer 2 (Syntactic), Layer 3 (Micro-Grid).
3
Ed25519 Signing
Every copy is signed with asymmetric cryptographic keypair.
4
Blockchain Ledger
Immutable append-only blocks with SHA-256 Merkle root trees.
5
Center Custody
Physical dispatch and vault handovers cryptographically recorded.
6
Leak Occurrence
Leaked text snippet, screenshot, or scan surfaces during exam.
7
Forensic Attribution
Tracer extracts copy ID, queries ledger, and pinpoints responsible custodian.
